FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS // KNOWLEDGE BASE
Will my QR code stop working after 14 days like other generators? +

No, absolutely not. Many commercial QR services secretly create dynamic redirect links that expire after 14 days unless you pay a monthly subscription. Link to QR generates 100% permanent static QR codes. Your data is encoded directly into the black-and-white matrix, meaning it will work forever with zero expiration dates, zero redirects, and zero recurring fees.

Is Link to QR completely free, or are there hidden scan limits? +

Link to QR is 100% free with unlimited scans and unlimited generations. There are no paywalls, hidden fees, watermark logos, subscriptions, or scan limits. You can use it freely for personal, professional, or commercial projects.

Is my data private and confidential? +

Yes. All QR code generation occurs 100% client-side inside your web browser. No URLs, Wi-Fi passwords, phone numbers, or text payloads are ever sent across the network or stored on our servers.

How do I make a Wi-Fi QR code for guests to connect instantly? +

Click the WI-FI tab above, enter your network name (SSID) and password. When guests scan the code using their iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens, their phone prompts them to join the Wi-Fi network in one tap without typing passwords.

Can I customize colors without breaking scanner readability? +

Yes! The fundamental rule for QR scannability is high contrast: always keep the QR pattern color noticeably darker than the background (such as black, dark navy, or dark charcoal on a white or light background). Avoid light QR patterns on light backgrounds or low-contrast combinations.

Can I print these QR codes on business cards, menus, or merchandise? +

Yes. In the SIZE dropdown, choose 800 px Print or 500 px HD. These export high-DPI, pixel-crisp PNG images with built-in quiet-zone padding specifically engineered for professional physical printing.

Can I generate QR codes for Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or WhatsApp? +

Yes. Select the URL tab and paste your social media profile, channel, or WhatsApp chat link (e.g. https://instagram.com/username or https://wa.me/number). The resulting QR code immediately routes scanners to your profile or chat.

What is the Error Correction level and which should I choose? +

QR codes utilize Reed-Solomon error correction to remain scannable even if partly damaged or dirty. High (30%) allows up to 30% of the code to be recovered, making it ideal for printed materials like restaurant menus and posters. Medium (15%) creates a slightly less dense pattern that is quicker to scan on screens.
